# Field textures

Both sources are released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication); no
attribution is required, but it is recorded here. Retrieved 24 October 2026.

| File | Source | Map |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `grass_color.jpg`, `grass_normal.jpg` | [ambientCG Grass004](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Grass004), 1K JPG | Color, NormalGL |
| `furrows_color.jpg`, `furrows_normal.jpg` | [Poly Haven farm_furrows](https://polyhaven.com/a/farm_furrows) by Amal Kumar, 1K JPG | diff, nor_gl |

The 1024×1024 maps were re-encoded with `sips` at JPEG quality 80 (colour) and
85 (normal) to reduce size. Their import settings generate mipmaps. The field
shader tiles the furrow texture once per plot and the grass texture across two
plots. These are modern photographs and scans, not evidence of medieval fields.

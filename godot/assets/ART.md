# Ștefan generated menu artwork — 24 October 2026

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Original generated outputs were
copied into this project without altering their pixels. The imagery is an artistic
medieval Moldavian interpretation, not a documented reconstruction.

- `game-icon.png`: square opaque gold aurochs shield on deep burgundy, used by
  `application/config/icon` in `project.godot`.
- `menu-background.png`: landscape fortress/valley painting, used by the menu's
  Background TextureRect with aspect-preserving crop.
- `menu-ornament.svg`: the existing code-drawn gold frame and emblem, separated
  from the previous opaque parchment so they can overlay the painting.

The title and Play remain actual Godot controls; no text is baked into the art.
A 22% darkening layer helps retain contrast. The previous opaque `menu.svg` was
removed after its decoration was retained in `menu-ornament.svg`.

## Field action icons

`actions/*.svg` are original, hand-authored transparent vector illustrations,
64×64 with a shared antique-gold palette. Plow uses a wheeled plow silhouette;
Harvest uses a broad sickle blade and wrapped grip. Wheat has plump paired
kernels, barley long awns, rye a slender alternating ear, oats hanging hulls,
and millet clustered round grains. The wheel draws them at 46 logical pixels;
names remain separate text controls/drawing. The bottom panel reuses these at
36 logical pixels, with names and amounts in tooltips. Its additional
`actions.svg`, `ledger.svg`, `inventory.svg` and `wiki.svg` depict a segmented
wheel, clasped ledger, grain sack and open reference book in the same palette. These are artistic game symbols,
not verified reconstructions of period tools or botanical illustrations.

Ștefan wiki

Ștefan is a farming and strategy game set on a 16 by 16 field of plots. These pages explain the seasons, the weather and how to grow grain. Open them in the game with F1 or the Wiki button.

The year

A year has four seasons of three months. Each month has four weeks of seven days, so a year is 336 days. The game starts on the first day of spring. At normal speed a day lasts two seconds.

Every day has its own weather: clear, cloudy, rain, storm or snow. Temperatures follow the modern climate of Chișinău, from about −1.5°C in midwinter to 22.5°C in midsummer, with warm, cold, wet and dry spells that last several days. Snow only falls at 1°C or colder.

Working the land

  • Plow grass, stubble or dead crops to make a field. The ground is frozen in winter.
  • Sow a plowed field with a crop during that crop's sowing months. Sowing uses the crop's own grain from the inventory as seed.
  • Harvest a ripe crop. Its grain goes to the inventory and the field is left as stubble, which must be plowed again before sowing.

Right-click land to open its action wheel: Plow for grass, stubble or dead crops; a separate icon for each available crop on plowed land; Harvest for ripe crops. Only actions possible now appear, with plot counts and seed costs or harvest yields. A growing crop or unavailable work shows its reason in the centre. Right-clicking a selected plot keeps the group, so mixed land offers actions for each eligible part. Click a segment, or use arrows/Tab and Enter. Escape or an outside click closes the wheel. Choosing a crop sows it and remembers it for Sow and F. Time keeps running and the wheel refreshes as days change.

The farm panel shows a button for each action the selected plots allow, with how many plots and how much grain it costs or brings. Plowed land stays plowed. Stubble and dead crops left alone turn back to grass after about 40 warm days; cold days count less and winter days not at all.

How crops grow

A crop grows each day the temperature is above its base, fastest at its best temperature. Rain helps and sunny days dry it out: oats gain the most from rain, and millet barely minds a dry spell. A crop dies on any day at or below its frost limit, whether it is still growing or already ripe, so harvest before the frost. Wheat and rye are winter crops: sown in autumn, they grow a little, wait out the winter and ripen the next summer.

Typically barley ripens in early summer, oats in mid to late summer, millet in late summer or early autumn, rye the next early summer and wheat after it. About one winter in five is cold enough to kill wheat. You start with 10 grain each of barley, oats, wheat and rye, and 6 of millet.

Soil

Every harvest wears the soil: barley and millet take 30% of its fertility, oats and rye 25% and wheat 35%. Worn soil gives a smaller harvest, though oats and rye mind it least. Land without a crop recovers 25% a year. Grow barley or millet on the same plot every year and its harvest halves within about nine years; wheat wears the soil out faster, oats and rye more slowly. Let a plot rest one year in three and a spring crop's harvest holds.

Ledger and inventory

  • The ledger (L) has three tabs. Fields shows what is growing by crop, with growth and soil. Years shows each year's sown, frost-killed and harvested plots and the grain gained after seed; hover a year for each crop. Crops lists each crop's needs.
  • The inventory (I) shows each crop's grain in store, how many plots it can sow and what this year has sown and harvested.

A first year

  1. In early spring, plow a few plots and sow barley or oats.
  2. Late in spring, sow millet on another plowed field.
  3. Harvest through the summer as each crop ripens.
  4. In autumn, plow the stubble and sow wheat or rye for next year.
  5. Rest some land each year to keep the soil fertile.

These crops and numbers are game choices, not verified history for fifteenth-century Moldavia. The weather follows today's climate, not a reconstruction of medieval weather.

Sound

Use Sound at the top right, or F10, in the menu or field. Mute all sound or adjust Master, Weather and Music. Use arrow keys on a focused volume slider; Escape closes the panel. Preferences are remembered. Wind, rain and quiet winter ambience blend as weather changes; thunder follows lightning. Pausing or speeding the calendar keeps sound at its natural speed.
